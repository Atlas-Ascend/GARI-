# Data and Documentation Licensing

## Software

Original software in this repository is licensed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` unless a file states otherwise.

## Original documentation

Original project documentation may be reused under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, subject to third-party rights and legal review.

## Public records and third-party material

No blanket repository license is asserted over government records, nonprofit filings, audit reports, contracts, meeting packets, photographs, trademarks, third-party datasets or other source materials.

Each source retains its own legal status, terms, copyright, privacy limitations and access conditions. Inclusion of a citation, locator, metadata record or hash does not transfer ownership or grant permission to republish the underlying source.

## Evidence storage rule

Where redistribution is unnecessary or legally uncertain, LANTERN stores only the source locator, provenance metadata, hash, citation location and analytical record. Contributors must not upload third-party source documents merely because they are publicly viewable.

## Personal and confidential data

No license authorizes collection or publication of client-level HMIS, medical, domestic-violence, shelter-history, Social Security, benefit-account or confidential-source information.
