# GARI LANTERN Governance Constitution

## Mission

Determine how publicly identifiable resources move through institutional systems, what obligations accompany those resources, what services and outcomes are reported, and where evidence remains incomplete, conflicting or unresolved.

The system must be able to discover misconduct, mismanagement, defective government contracting, legitimate performance, underfunding, unsupported allegations or an inconclusive record.

## Non-negotiable laws

1. No claim without cited evidence.
2. No one approves their own material claim.
3. Missing data is a gap, not missing money.
4. Awards, obligations, payments and expenditures remain distinct.
5. No autonomous GARI publication or entity merge.
6. No corruption score, fraud probability or public suspicion ranking.
7. No client-level HMIS, health, domestic-violence or shelter-history data.
8. No confidential tip intake during Genesis.
9. No regional outcome attributed to one provider without a defensible attribution design.
10. Corrections preserve the original public statement.
11. No high-risk claim without factual, accounting, adversarial, privacy, legal and substantive review.
12. A finding of no supported misconduct is a valid successful result.

## Decision roles

| Role | Authority | Prohibition |
|---|---|---|
| Public Interest Commissioner | Approves mission scope and conflicts | Cannot predetermine a finding |
| Director of Evidence Integrity | Defines evidence standards and certifies manifests | Cannot lead the case being certified |
| Lead Case Investigator | Builds the case file and unresolved-question register | Cannot fact-check or release their own finding |
| Entity Resolution Examiner | Resolves organizations and people | Cannot silently merge uncertain identities |
| Forensic Nonprofit Accountant | Interprets financial statements and money events | Cannot provide legal publication approval |
| Outcome Measurement Scientist | Tests denominator, period and attribution validity | Cannot infer causal impact from regional association |
| Claim-Level Fact Checker | Rebuilds each claim from original sources | Cannot trust the investigator's summary as evidence |
| Adversarial Evidence Examiner | Attempts to defeat the proposed conclusion | Cannot suppress evidence that strengthens it |
| Privacy Officer | Controls minimization and release of personal information | Cannot waive protections for convenience |
| Publication Counsel | Reviews jurisdiction-specific publication risk | Cannot substitute for factual verification |
| Evidence Publication Editor | Releases only what the evidence supports | Cannot inflate wording for attention |
| Corrections Registrar | Maintains appeals and append-only corrections | Cannot erase the original history |

A small team may hold multiple roles, but conflicting actions on the same claim are prohibited.

## Case selection

Cases are prioritized by public funds involved, community consequence, evidence availability, actionability, recurrence, severity, geographic equity, conflict risk and resource cost. Popularity, ideology, personal retaliation and spectacle are invalid selection criteria.

## Conflict rule for LSC-OR-CLATSOP-001

The founder has direct lived and service-system contact with Clatsop County providers. That experience may identify questions, records gaps and operational contradictions. It cannot serve as sole financial proof, independent verification or final publication authority.

## Claim states

```text
internal lead
→ research hypothesis
→ verified internal finding
→ counsel-reviewed finding where required
→ release-approved
→ published
→ corrected or withdrawn when necessary
```

A claim cannot skip states.

## Right of reply

Material findings must be presented with the relevant dates, amounts, records and clear questions. New evidence supplied by a subject returns through preservation, extraction, accounting, fact-checking and adversarial review. A response is not decorative text appended after a predetermined conclusion.

## Corrections

Every material correction records the original statement, corrected statement, reason, discovery date, correction date, reviewer, affected releases and supersession chain. Git history alone is not the public correction mechanism.

## Community governance

Lived-experience participation must be paid when institutional resources permit. Community reviewers help select questions, test plain-language reports and identify harms. They are not asked to expose private client information or certify accounting outside their expertise.

## Funding independence

Contributors and funders may not condition support on investigating, clearing, targeting or suppressing a named subject. Material financial, political, employment, litigation and personal conflicts must be disclosed.

## Genesis release rule

The initial website may publish the methodology, safe resolved identities, typed official funding summaries and explicit limitations. It has no authority to publish a misconduct finding.
