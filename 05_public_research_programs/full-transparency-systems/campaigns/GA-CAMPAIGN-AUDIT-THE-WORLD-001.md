## Campaign thesis

Ghost Atlas becomes recognizable through a repeatable capability:

> **Enter a system. Census it. Trace it. Model it. Test it. Expose the gaps between stated reality and evidenced reality. Route repair. Re-test. Publish the proof path. Repeat.**

The campaign is not a collection of unrelated investigations. It is one universal audit methodology instantiated repeatedly across different domains.

## Claim to fame

**Ghost Atlas builds transparent models of opaque systems and produces replayable evidence paths from observation to governance verdict.**

The signature capability is not domain omniscience. It is domain translation into a stable audit grammar:

`SYSTEM + SOURCES + ACTORS + CLAIMS + EVENTS + RULES + CONTROLS + INCENTIVES + EVIDENCE + TESTS + FINDINGS + REMEDIES + RECEIPTS + DELTAS`.

Domain expertise enters through control packs and specialist review. The audit spine remains constant.

## Campaign doctrine

`EVIDENCE FIRST -> STRUCTURE SECOND -> PATTERN THIRD -> INTERPRETATION FOURTH -> REMEDIATION FIFTH -> PROOF ALWAYS`

The original FTS public doctrine remains: **Map structure. Do not chase outrage.**

## The universal audit box

Every deployable FTS audit kit contains:

1. **Scope Freezer** — target, authority, time, access and exposure boundaries.
2. **System Census** — components, actors, interfaces, dependencies, decision rights.
3. **Source Harvester** — connectors and field capture with provenance.
4. **Universal Normalizer** — maps domain-native objects into the FTS schema.
5. **Chronology Engine** — reconstructs events and state transitions.
6. **Claim-Evidence Engine** — binds assertions to sources and evidence states.
7. **Power/Information Engine** — maps who knows, decides, benefits, blocks, bears impact.
8. **Control-Pack Loader** — loads laws, standards, policies, contracts, specs, acceptance criteria.
9. **Contradiction Engine** — detects conflicts, missing links, false completion, policy/practice gaps.
10. **Test Harness** — executes documentary, logical, technical, computational, procedural or physical tests.
11. **Ma'at Gate** — converts evidence into traceable findings and verdicts.
12. **Remediation Compiler** — finding -> Packet OS task -> owner -> acceptance test -> proof requirement.
13. **Execution Bridge** — Workforce/PROMETHEUS/MetaForge/EDEN route authorized repairs.
14. **Re-test Engine** — proves whether state actually changed.
15. **Medusa Gate** — private/reviewer/public classification and redaction.
16. **Proof Exporter** — Thoth archive, ProofGrid index, CaseGraph/reviewer portal, reproducibility pack.
17. **Delta Engine** — re-audit and regression tracking.

## CaseGraph role

CaseGraph is the first hard vertical proof that the universal model can present a complex evidence-rich matter to an investigator/reviewer. It becomes an adapter/projection, not the boundary of FTS.

A future software audit, nonprofit audit, AI audit, or infrastructure audit may render a different front end while using the same core object IDs, evidence lineage, control tests, and verdict states.

## Seshat role

Seshat Atlas becomes the system cartographer for every audit:

- what exists;
- what depends on what;
- what state each object is in;
- what changed since the prior audit;
- what remains blind or unresolved.

## Aletheia role

Aletheia / ProofGrid answers:

- what evidence supports this;
- how strong and complete is the chain;
- where is the source;
- what test produced this result;
- what receipt proves the transition.

## Ma'at role

Ma'at / SECA / DevOS answers:

- what control applied;
- what evidence was sufficient;
- what failed;
- what remains unresolved;
- what remedy is required;
- whether the remedy survived re-test;
- whether the audit may be promoted.

## Vishvarupa role

Vishvarupa is the total-form view across simultaneous audit campaigns. It does not perform every audit step. It sees the complete estate-wide portfolio of targets, dependencies, conflicts, receipts, verdicts, and next frontiers.
