## Mission

Full Transparency Systems (FTS) is the Ghost Atlas domain-agnostic audit program for turning opaque systems into evidence-bearing, replayable models of how they actually operate.

FTS accepts a bounded system under review, freezes the audit scope, inventories the system, reconstructs chronology and state, maps actors/power/incentives/information flow, binds claims to evidence, loads applicable control frameworks, tests contradictions and failure modes, routes remediation, re-tests outcomes, and emits a public/private proof package whose lineage can be inspected later.

FTS is not a new estate organ. It is a campaign and protocol layer that composes the existing organism.

## Core audit questions

1. What is the system and what does it claim to do?
2. What actually happened or is happening?
3. Who has power, responsibility, incentives, access, and decision rights?
4. What information enters, moves through, or disappears inside the system?
5. Which claims are supported, contradicted, unresolved, retracted, or superseded?
6. Which controls, laws, policies, contracts, standards, requirements, or acceptance criteria apply?
7. Where does observed behavior diverge from stated purpose or required behavior?
8. What human, operational, technical, financial, institutional, or public impact follows?
9. What remediation would change the state rather than merely explain it?
10. What test would prove the remediation actually worked?
11. What may be publicly exposed, and what remains protected?
12. Can another reviewer reproduce the reasoning path from source to verdict?
